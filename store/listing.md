## Full description (2448/4000)

```text
ShroomLock is an app blocker that teaches you something. Turn every urge to scroll into a five-second mushroom lesson.

Pick the apps that eat your time, such as social media, video or games. When you open one, ShroomLock first shows a photo of a mushroom you have learned, with four names to choose from.

HOW IT WORKS
• Right answer: the app opens for the time you chose (10 minutes by default).
• Wrong answer: a short pause shows the right name and how to tell the two apart, so tapping at random doesn't pay off.
• Changed your mind? "I don't need this app right now" is always one tap away, and a few emergency unlocks a day are there when you really need one.

LEARN A FEW MUSHROOMS A DAY
• A short daily lesson introduces new mushrooms, then a quick exam checks them.
• Spaced review brings each mushroom back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days until you have mastered it.
• Wrong answers are real look-alikes, such as chanterelle vs jack-o’-lantern, morel vs false morel, parasol vs false parasol, so you learn the differences that matter.
• Field clues on every card: cap, underside, stem, spore print, habitat and season, and the one feature that tells it apart. Deadly species are clearly marked.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 71 mushrooms: gilled mushrooms, boletes, brackets, chanterelles, morels, puffballs and more, each with real photos.
• A daily streak, a collection with milestones, and your Mycology IQ.
• Light and dark mode.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE
• Free: Lock up to 2 apps and learn the 35 gilled mushrooms.
• ShroomLock Plus (one-time purchase, no subscription): Unlimited locked apps and all 71 mushrooms, including boletes, brackets and other fungi.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

PERMISSIONS
Locking apps is the core feature of ShroomLock. To do it, it asks for:
• Usage access, to see which app is in front.
• Display over other apps, to show the question over a locked app.
• A notification, so Android keeps the lock running.
This information never leaves your phone.

SAFETY
ShroomLock teaches recognition, not foraging. Never eat a wild mushroom because an app or a photo said it is edible. Have every find checked in person by an expert.

Photos come from iNaturalist under Creative Commons licences; every photo is credited in the app.

Website: https://www.sinadehesh.com/shroomlock/
Privacy policy: https://www.sinadehesh.com/shroomlock/privacy/
```
